Privacy & Policies

How Mahadev Fitness Club handles your data, who can have an account, and who to contact. Written in plain words. Last updated 17 November 2026.

1What data we collect and why

We collect only what a gym needs to run your membership. We never sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

DataWhy we need it
Name and mobile numberTo identify you as a member, contact you and send fee reminders on WhatsApp.
Email, Google picture and Google ID (if you sign in with Google)To let you log in without a password. We never see your Google password.
Photo (optional)So gym staff can recognise you.
Plan (with or without cardio), fees, last fee dateTo work out your fee due date and show your membership status.
Attendance (date and time of your QR scan)To record your visits and show your streak.
Payment details: amount, UPI reference number (UTR), timeTo confirm that you paid. We never see your UPI PIN, card or bank login.
Enquiry form detailsTo reply to you. The form opens your email app and sends the mail to the owner. The website does not store it.

We do not collect Aadhaar, PAN, bank account numbers, health records or your location.

2Who can have an account
Accounts are for gym members only.
  • Only people who are members of Mahadev Fitness Club may log in and create an account on this website.
  • We take this precaution to protect our members' data and our attendance records.
  • If anyone unusual creates an account, we check it against our member records. If the person is not in our records, we review it and delete the account immediately.
  • If you are a real member and your account was removed by mistake, contact the gym and we will restore it after checking.
3Age and parent consent
  • 18 and above: you may join and create your own account.
  • Under 18: you need your parent's or guardian's consent first. The parent or guardian must also give us proof of that consent.

Any one of these is accepted as proof:

  • A written application or consent letter from the parent or guardian.
  • A confirmation phone call from the parent or guardian to the gym, or from the gym to them.
  • Any other proof the gym finds reliable.

We do not track children, show them ads, or send them promotions. If we find we hold a child's data without parent consent, we delete it.

4Your consent and your rights
  • Consent: we ask for your agreement to this policy when you join or sign up. We never assume it for you. If the owner adds you at the gym desk, we tell you what we collect and take your agreement there.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, as easily as you gave it. Tell a grievance officer below.
  • Access and correction: ask what we hold about you, or ask us to fix your details.
  • Deletion: we act on your request within 7 days. Fee payment records are kept without your name, phone or photo, because accounts and tax law require this.
  • Complaints: see the grievance officers below. We reply within 15 days and never later than 90 days.
  • Nominee: you may name a person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.

Fee reminders and gym notices are service messages. We send offers only if you separately agree.

5How long we keep your data
DataHow long
Profile and attendanceWe keep your data only while you are a member of the gym. If your plan ends and you do not renew within 30 days, we treat you as no longer a member and remove your data from our database.
Accounts that never became a paid membershipUnusual accounts that are not in our member records are deleted immediately after review. Other accounts with no plan are removed after 30 days.
Payment recordsUp to 8 years, as accounts and tax law require. Kept without your name, phone, email or photo after you delete your account.
Enquiry emailsUp to 12 months.
Owner alerts (Telegram)Kept in the owner's private chat and cleared at least once a year.
6Who we share data with
Your data stays with us. We keep it with us and do not share it with anyone. It is visible only in our admin section.
  • We do not share your data with any other person, company or advertiser. We do not sell or rent it.
  • Only the owner and authorised staff in the admin section can see it.
  • To run the system we use technical services that store or carry data on our behalf, only to provide that service to us: Google (sign-in, fonts, map), Render and Neon (hosting and database), GitHub (private photo store), Telegram (alerts to the owner) and WhatsApp (the owner sends fee reminders from his own phone).
  • We give data to an authority only if a law or court requires it. Some of these services keep servers outside India.
7Refund policy
No refund on any plan.
  • We do not refund fees for any month, whether you take the 1, 3, 6 or 12 month plan (with or without cardio).
  • This applies if you leave in between, stop coming, or for any other reason. There is no fee refund.
  • Fees cannot be transferred to another person.
  • For further details, contact Ashish Singh: +91 9580752023, Ashishrajsngh718@gmail.com.
8Security and cookies
  • The website uses HTTPS (an encrypted connection).
  • Members sign in with Google, so we hold no member passwords.
  • Only the owner and authorised staff can open the admin area, behind a password.
  • Member photos are shown only to staff and to the member themself.
  • If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.
  • Cookies: we use one login cookie and a theme setting (light or dark). No advertising or tracking cookies. The Google map and fonts load from Google, which may receive your IP address.
9Terms & conditions in short
  • Fees: plans are 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, with or without cardio (₹500 per month extra). Your due date stays on the same day of the month. Paying late does not give extra days. In February the due date moves to the last day of the month, then returns to the normal date.
  • UPI payments count as paid only after the owner confirms the UTR you enter. A false UTR can end your membership.
  • No refunds: see the Refund policy above. No fee is refunded for a 1, 3, 6 or 12 month plan.
  • Health: exercise has a risk of injury. Check with a doctor if you have a medical condition, and tell the trainers about any injury.
  • Conduct: respect staff and members. Mark attendance only for yourself.
  • Law: Indian law applies, with courts in Lucknow. Your consumer rights are not affected.
