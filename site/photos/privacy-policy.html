Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-17

In short: we collect only what a gym needs: who you are, your plan, your fees and your attendance. We use it only to run your membership. We never sell it. We keep it only while you are a member. When you stop being a member, we remove it from our database. Your data stays with us and is not shared with anyone. You can ask us to delete it any time.

1. Who we are

Mahadev Fitness Club, Gupta Market, Prem Nagar, Muslim Nagar, Alambagh, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226005. The gym owner, Ashish Singh, decides why and how your data is used (the "Data Fiduciary" under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). The website and app are built and looked after by Prakhar Sharma, who handles your data only for the gym and only as the gym instructs.

2. What data we collect, and why

3. Your consent

When you join, or sign up online, we ask for your agreement to this policy. We never assume it for you. If the owner adds you at the gym desk, we tell you what we collect and take your agreement there. Fee reminders and gym notices are service messages. We will not send you offers or promotions unless you separately agree.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: contact a grievance officer (see section 11). If you withdraw, we can no longer run your membership and we will delete your data as described below.

4. Who we share your data with

Your data stays with us. We do not share it with any other person, company or advertiser, and we do not sell or rent it. Inside the system, only the owner and authorised staff in the admin section can see it.

To run the gym system we use technical services that store or carry your data on our behalf, and only to provide that service to us:

  • Google: sign-in, fonts and the map on our home page.
  • Render: hosts the website and app.
  • Neon: stores the member database.
  • GitHub: stores member photos in a private repository that only the gym controls.
  • Telegram: the owner gets an alert when someone marks attendance, signs up or pays. The alert contains your name and may contain your phone number, time and amount.
  • WhatsApp: the owner sends fee reminders from his own phone, using your mobile number.

We give data to an authority only if a law, court or government order requires it. Some of these services keep servers outside India, so your data may be processed outside India.

6. Your rights

  • Access: ask what data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Correction: ask us to change your name, phone or plan details.
  • Deletion: ask a grievance officer. We act on it within 7 days.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, see section 3.
  • Complaint: contact a grievance officer (section 11). We reply within 15 days and never later than 90 days.
  • Nominate someone: you may nominate a person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.

7. Who can have an account

Accounts on this website are for gym members only. We take this precaution to protect our members' data. If anyone unusual creates an account, we check it against our member records. If the person is not in our records, we review it and delete the account immediately. If you are a real member and your account was removed by mistake, contact the gym and we will restore it after checking.

8. Age and parent consent

If you are 18 or older, you may join and create your own account. If you are under 18, you need your parent's or guardian's consent first, and the parent or guardian must give the gym proof of it. Any one of these is accepted:

  • a written application or consent letter from the parent or guardian,
  • a confirmation call from the parent or guardian to the gym, or from the gym to them, or
  • any other proof the gym finds reliable.

We ask you to confirm your age when you join. We do not track children, show them ads, or send them promotions. If we find we hold a child's data without parent consent, we delete it.

9. Security

  • The website uses HTTPS (an encrypted connection).
  • Members sign in with Google, so we hold no member passwords.
  • Only the owner and authorised staff can open the admin area, behind a password.
  • Member photos are in a private store and are shown only to staff and to the member themself.

If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

10. Cookies and similar tools

We use only what is needed to run the site: one login (session) cookie, and a small browser setting that remembers your light or dark theme. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The Google map and Google Fonts on the home page are loaded from Google, which may receive your IP address and set its own cookies. See Google's privacy policy for details.
